Why AI Bias Matters

Traditional software bugs cause system failures. AI bias causes human harm through unfair, discriminatory outcomes.

Healthcare AI

Misdiagnosis based on gender or ethnicity

Financial AI

Unfair loan denials based on zip code

Hiring AI

Resume screening that favors certain demographics

Types of AI Bias

Data Bias

Training data doesn't represent real-world diversity

Example: Facial recognition trained primarily on light-skinned males

Label Bias

Human labelers inject their own biases into training data

Example: Resumes with "ethnic" names rated lower

Historical Bias

Past discrimination patterns are learned and perpetuated

Example: Loan approval models replicating redlining

Feedback Loop Bias

AI predictions influence future data, reinforcing bias

Example: Recommendation systems creating filter bubbles
